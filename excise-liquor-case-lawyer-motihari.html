About prohibition in Bihar

Since April 2016, the Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act has banned the manufacture, sale, transport and consumption of liquor in Bihar. Offences are tried by designated Special Courts, and vehicles and premises connected with an offence can be seized and made subject to confiscation proceedings, which have their own remedies and time limits.

Common questions

Which court hears liquor cases in Motihari?

Cases under the Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act are heard by the designated Special Court (Excise) at the Civil Court, Motihari.

Can a vehicle seized in a liquor case be released?

A vehicle seized in an excise case is usually subject to confiscation proceedings under the Act. Whether and how it can be released depends on the stage of those proceedings and the facts of the case.
