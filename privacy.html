What this website collects

This is a static information website. It has no forms, accounts or tracking cookies, and it does not collect your name, phone number or other personal data.

The website stores a single setting in your browser to remember that you accepted the disclaimer, so it is not shown again on every page. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser’s site data.
