Common questions

What does a criminal lawyer in Motihari do?

A criminal lawyer represents people at each stage of a criminal case: after an FIR or complaint, at arrest and remand, in bail applications, at trial, and in appeals or revisions. In Motihari these cases are heard at the Civil Court.

Which law applies to a criminal case today?

Offences committed on or after 1 July 2024 fall under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), with procedure under the BNSS. Offences before that date are generally governed by the IPC.
