About the practice

Focused on criminal law in East Champaran

Inspire Advocacy is the chamber of Advocate Rupesh Kumar Thakur, who practises criminal law at the courts in Motihari, the district headquarters of East Champaran, Bihar.

Criminal cases move quickly at the start: an arrest, a first remand, a bail hearing, or the seizure of a vehicle. The practice is built around those stages, as well as the longer work of a trial. Matters are handled in Hindi, Bhojpuri and English, and the advocate explains each step of the procedure to the client and their family.

Since 1 July 2024, criminal cases are governed by the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA). Cases about events before that date can still involve the IPC and CrPC, so both sets of law remain part of everyday practice.

New criminal laws

The 2023 codes, at a glance

Three new laws replaced the colonial-era codes on 1 July 2024. Familiar section numbers have changed, which matters when reading an FIR or a court order.

Old lawNew law
Indian Penal Code, 1860Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)
Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)
Indian Evidence Act, 1872Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)

Know your rights

If someone in your family is arrested

  • They must be told the grounds of arrest and whether the offence is bailable (BNSS s. 47).
  • A relative or friend must be informed of the arrest (BNSS s. 48).
  • They must be produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours (BNSS s. 58; Article 22 of the Constitution).
  • They may meet an advocate of their choice during interrogation (BNSS s. 38).
  • Free legal aid is available through the District Legal Services Authority. NALSA helpline: 15100.
