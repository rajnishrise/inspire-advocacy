Bail & Anticipatory Bail

BNSS ss. 478–483, 187(3) · formerly CrPC ss. 436–439, 167(2)

Applications for release from custody and for protection from arrest, from the Magistrate’s court to the Court of Session.

  • Regular bail before the Magistrate and the Sessions Court (BNSS ss. 480 & 483)
  • Anticipatory bail when arrest is apprehended (BNSS s. 482)
  • Interim bail and bail pending trial
  • Default (statutory) bail where the charge-sheet is not filed in time (BNSS s. 187(3))
  • Bail under special statutes with stricter conditions, such as the NDPS Act (s. 37) and the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act
  • Modification of bail conditions, and cancellation proceedings
  • Surety and bail-bond formalities after an order is passed

Criminal Trials & Defence

BNS 2023 · BNSS 2023 · BSA 2023

Representation through each stage of a criminal case, from cognisance and charge to evidence, arguments and judgment.

  • Sessions trials (offences such as murder, attempt to murder and dowry death)
  • Warrant and summons trials before Judicial Magistrates
  • Discharge applications and framing of charge
  • Cross-examination of prosecution witnesses and defence evidence
  • Matters on the old codes (IPC/CrPC) for offences committed before 1 July 2024
  • Plea bargaining and compounding where the law permits

FIR, Complaint & Investigation Stage

BNSS ss. 173, 175, 223 · formerly CrPC ss. 154, 156(3), 200

Help at the early stage, which often shapes the rest of a case.

  • Drafting and filing complaints, including complaint cases before the Magistrate (BNSS s. 223)
  • Applications to direct registration of an FIR and investigation (BNSS s. 175(3))
  • Responding to notices of appearance from the police (BNSS s. 35(3), formerly CrPC s. 41A)
  • Protest petitions against a closure report
  • Representation for persons named in an FIR during investigation

Bihar Prohibition & Excise Act

Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act, 2016 (as amended)

Bihar has had total prohibition since 2016. Cases under this Act are heard by designated Special Courts and carry their own procedure.

  • Bail in prohibition cases before the Special Court (Excise)
  • Defence at trial in liquor recovery, consumption and transport cases
  • Confiscation proceedings, and release of seized vehicles and premises
  • Representation for first-time consumption offences under the amended provisions

Vehicle & Property Release

BNSS ss. 497, 503 · formerly CrPC ss. 451, 457

Seized vehicles and property can deteriorate while a case is pending. The law allows the court to order interim custody (zimmanama) in many cases.

  • Release of vehicles seized in accident, theft and excise cases
  • Interim custody (supurdnama/zimmanama) of seized property
  • Release of mobile phones, documents, cash and other articles
  • Applications where confiscation proceedings are pending before an authority

NDPS & Special Statutes

NDPS Act, 1985 · Arms Act, 1959 · SC/ST Act, 1989 · POCSO Act, 2012

Offences under special laws are tried by special courts and often carry stricter bail conditions and procedural safeguards.

  • Narcotic Drugs and Psychotropic Substances (NDPS) Act cases
  • Arms Act and Explosive Substances Act cases
  • Cases under the SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act
  • Cases before the Special POCSO Court
  • Customs, Foreigners Act and other cases arising near the Indo-Nepal border

Matrimonial & Dowry-Related Offences

BNS ss. 80, 85, 86 · Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 · DV Act, 2005

Criminal cases arising from family disputes, handled with care for everyone involved.

  • Cases of cruelty by husband or relatives (BNS ss. 85 & 86, formerly IPC s. 498A)
  • Dowry death and Dowry Prohibition Act cases
  • Proceedings under the Protection of Women from Domestic Violence Act, 2005
  • Maintenance proceedings for wives, children and parents (BNSS s. 144, formerly CrPC s. 125)
  • Mediation and settlement where the law allows

Cheque Bounce & Financial Offences

Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, s. 138 · BNS s. 318

Complaints and defence in dishonour-of-cheque cases, and related cheating and breach-of-trust cases.

  • Legal notice and complaint under s. 138 of the NI Act
  • Defence of accused persons in cheque-dishonour cases
  • Cheating and criminal breach of trust (BNS ss. 316 & 318, formerly IPC ss. 406 & 420)
  • Compounding and settlement of NI Act cases

Cyber Crime

Information Technology Act, 2000 · BNS

Cases involving online fraud, impersonation and harassment.

  • Online banking and UPI fraud cases
  • Social-media harassment, defamation and impersonation
  • Representation for persons accused under the IT Act
  • Release of seized phones and devices

Cyber crime cases in Motihari: more information →

Juvenile Justice

Juvenile Justice (Care and Protection of Children) Act, 2015

Children in conflict with the law are dealt with by the Juvenile Justice Board, not the regular criminal court.

  • Bail and release of children in conflict with the law
  • Age determination inquiries
  • Proceedings before the Juvenile Justice Board, East Champaran

Revisions, Appeals & Other Remedies

BNSS ss. 415, 438 · formerly CrPC ss. 374, 397

Challenging orders and judgments of subordinate criminal courts.

  • Criminal revisions before the Court of Session
  • Criminal appeals against conviction from Magistrates’ courts
  • Applications for suspension of sentence
  • Guidance on remedies that lie before the Patna High Court, such as quashing (BNSS s. 528, formerly CrPC s. 482)
