About the advocate

Advocate Rupesh Kumar Thakur

Criminal law practitioner at the Civil Court, Motihari, East Champaran, Bihar.

Profile

Advocate Rupesh Kumar Thakur is a criminal law practitioner based in Motihari, Bihar, and the founder of Inspire Advocacy. His practice is concentrated in the criminal courts of East Champaran: the Court of the District & Sessions Judge, the courts of the Chief Judicial Magistrate and Judicial Magistrates, and the special courts that sit at Motihari.

His work spans bail and anticipatory bail, criminal trials, matters under the Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act, NDPS and other special statutes, and applications for the release of seized vehicles and property. He has been enrolled with the Bar Council of Bihar since 2013, holds a B.Sc. and an LL.B., and is a member of the Motihari Bar Association.

Approach

  • Clear explanation. Clients and their families are told plainly what stage the case is at, what comes next and what documents are needed.
  • Preparation. Each application is drafted from the case diary, FIR and documents on record, with the current law, including the 2023 codes.
  • Accessibility. Consultations in Hindi, Bhojpuri and English.
  • Confidentiality. What a client shares stays protected by professional privilege (BSA ss. 132–134).

Beyond Motihari

Matters that go to the High Court

The advocate’s practice is at the courts in Motihari. Some remedies, such as quashing of an FIR or proceedings (BNSS s. 528, formerly CrPC s. 482), bail after rejection by the Sessions Court, and criminal appeals from Sessions trials, lie before the Patna High Court. Where a matter needs to go to the High Court, clients are told when such a remedy is available and what it involves, so that they can engage counsel there.
