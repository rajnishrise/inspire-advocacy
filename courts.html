Courts & criminal procedure in East Champaran

Where criminal cases are heard at Motihari, how a case moves through the courts, and the section numbers that changed in 2024.

Courts at Motihari

Motihari is the headquarters of East Champaran district. The district judiciary sits at the Civil Court campus, where the following criminal forums are commonly involved:

  • Court of the District & Sessions Judge and Additional Sessions Judges: sessions trials, bail and anticipatory bail, criminal appeals and revisions.
  • Court of the Chief Judicial Magistrate and Judicial Magistrates: first production and remand, bail in Magistrate-triable cases, complaint cases, and release of property.
  • Special Courts: Excise (Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act), NDPS, POCSO and SC/ST (Prevention of Atrocities) Act cases.
  • Family Court: maintenance and related proceedings.
  • Juvenile Justice Board: children in conflict with the law.

Cause lists, case status and orders for the Motihari courts are published on the eCourts portal for East Champaran.

Procedure

How a criminal case moves

A simplified outline. Actual steps vary with the offence and the court.

FIR & investigation

An FIR is registered (BNSS s. 173), or a complaint is filed before the Magistrate. Police investigate, may arrest, and record statements.

Arrest, remand & bail

An arrested person is produced before a Magistrate within 24 hours. Bail may be sought before the Magistrate, then the Sessions Court.

Charge-sheet & cognisance

Police file a police report (charge-sheet) or closure report. The court takes cognisance and the case is committed to Sessions if required.

Charge, trial & judgment

Charges are framed or the accused is discharged. Evidence and arguments follow, then judgment, with appeal or revision available.

New criminal laws

Common section changes since 1 July 2024

The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS), Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita (BNSS) and Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam (BSA) came into force on 1 July 2024. Offences committed before that date generally remain governed by the IPC, and proceedings that were already pending continue under the old procedure (BNSS s. 531).

Bihar specific

Prohibition cases in Bihar

Total prohibition

The Bihar Prohibition and Excise Act, 2016 bans the manufacture, sale, transport and consumption of liquor across the State.

Special Courts

Offences under the Act are tried by designated Special Courts (Excise), which also hear bail applications in these cases.

Confiscation

Vehicles and premises connected with an offence may be seized and made subject to confiscation proceedings, which have their own remedies and time limits.
