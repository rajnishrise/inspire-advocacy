What to bring to a consultation

  • Copy of the FIR or complaint, and the case number if known
  • Any court orders, notices or summons received
  • Charge-sheet or police report, if filed
  • Seizure list, for vehicle or property release
  • Identity proof of the accused or applicant
  • Documents of proposed sureties, for bail
  • Vehicle RC and insurance, for vehicle release
  • A short written timeline of events
